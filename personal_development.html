My Personal Development

Early in my career transition, I discovered a deep passion for data—after all, it’s the lifeblood of every business. Encouraged by my colleagues, I explored the world of data governance, joining the Data Management Community (DAMA) and diving into the DMBOK framework.

While governance was insightful, I craved a more hands-on approach. I took short coding courses and explored Azure, but going fully technical pulled me away from what truly excited me, the data itself.

In 2022, I started looking for a balance between strategy and technical expertise. With my children grown, I decided to formalise my knowledge, and in 2023, I began a degree in Data Science, specialising in AI and Machine Learning. Here, I’ve truly found my passion - using code to transform data into insights through compelling visualisations.

This journey has reinforced that personal growth isn’t about choosing one path—it’s about adapting, learning, and finding where your skills create the most impact.

Toby Beevers
